# Vouchers, memberships and billing – business rules (conduit model)

**Principle:** TapIt facilitates, it does not collect. No guest or end-user money ever passes through TapIt. Businesses sell their own memberships, vouchers, bookings and handle their own payments, refunds and terms. TapIt earns only from:
1. Referral commission (invoiced to the business)
2. Subscriptions (concierge plan, analytics, etc.)
3. Feature upsells (featured listing, vouchers/memberships module, trail pack)

## What businesses can run (as TapIt features they pay for)
- **Vouchers:** the business defines the offer, validity, limits and eligibility. The guest shows a code/QR; the business confirms redemption in its portal. TapIt records the event.
- **Memberships / VIP programmes:** the business defines tiers, perks and points, and sells them through its own checkout (external link or its own provider). TapIt shows the programme and records the referral. Points, if any, live in the business's system or its own tenant data, not a TapIt wallet.
- **Bookings:** guest is sent to the operator's booking page or WhatsApp with a referral code.

## Network-level features run by TapIt (no payments)
- Passport stamps and challenges (e.g. Kogelberg Explorer). Prizes are funded and fulfilled by sponsors or partners.
- Trip history and 3D replay.

## TapIt's own ledger (income only)
- Line types: commission, subscription, upsell, credit note.
- Append-only; corrections are credit notes, never edits.
- Invoices go to businesses monthly. VAT only on TapIt's own fees (confirm TapIt's VAT status with the accountant).

## The hard problem: verifying referral commission
TapIt does not see the sale, so commission depends on evidence. Use three signals before invoicing:
1. **Referral code or tagged link** carried from the TapIt page into the partner checkout (auto match where the partner supports it).
2. **Partner confirmation** in the portal ("this booking came from TapIt").
3. **Spot-checks:** TapIt may request proof for a sample; disputes are tracked.
Put reporting and audit rights in the partner agreement. Without them, commission is on trust.

## Legal notes (get advice, not legal advice)
- Because TapIt takes no guest payments, the VAT/refund/consumer-payment exposure is the business's, not TapIt's.
- TapIt's agreement with each business should say TapIt is a facilitator, the business is the seller of record, and the business owns its terms, refunds and compliance.
- Check how advertising and referral claims shown on TapIt pages are covered under consumer-protection rules.
- POPIA still applies to TapIt for guest data it holds (trips, location, passport).

## Open questions for the client
1. "Royalty" earlier meant what? Revenue share to the forum or partners (money owed by TapIt or to it), or member points? Under the conduit model this should be a share of TapIt's own income, invoiced or paid by TapIt, never of guest money.
2. Can partners add the referral code at their checkout, or must they confirm manually?
3. Who funds Passport prizes: sponsors, partners, or the forum?
4. Commission rate and terms: one rate, or negotiated per partner? Cancellation and chargeback handling?
