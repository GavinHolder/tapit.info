# TapIt – Client Discovery Questions

Deadline for pilot: **end of November 2026**. Answers marked ★ block design or build work this week.

## A. Pilot & content
1. ★ How many pilot sites are confirmed (name, town, type)? Who is the contact at each?
2. ★ Who will write each site's concierge content: the owner, the client, or us? (Drives the CMS editor scope.)
3. Languages needed at launch (EN / AF / DE / other)?
4. Can we get each pilot's logo, brand colours, 5–10 photos, Wi-Fi, breakfast/checkout times, house rules, WhatsApp number, Google Review link?
5. Is the Whale Coast Directory a site the client owns? Does it have an API, database or export we can use?
6. Who curates "things to do" (hikes, restaurants, events) for the pilot, and is there existing data (spreadsheet, brochure, site)?

## B. Brand & ownership
7. ★ Which legal entity owns TapIt (company name, reg. number, VAT status)? Is the business forum involved, and how?
8. ★ Who owns the domain, GitHub org, VPS and payment accounts?
9. Existing brand assets (logo, colours, fonts) for TapIt, or do we design them?
10. Tone: premium/adventurous, or friendly/local? Any competitor or site the client loves visually?

## C. Money (needed to design the ledger)
11. ★ What will pilot sites pay: setup fee, monthly fee, free, or sponsored? Any price already promised?
12. ★ Commission: fixed % per operator or per-booking negotiable? Who collects the guest's money: TapIt, or the operator directly?
13. Payouts: how often and by what method (EFT)? Does the client want TapIt to hold funds, or only invoice commission after the fact?
14. Is TapIt VAT-registered? Which invoices/statements must the system produce?
15. Royalties: what are they exactly (e.g. share to the forum or sponsors)? Who pays whom, and on what basis?
16. Has the client taken legal/accounting advice on acting as a booking agent or tour operator (including indemnity, cancellation, consumer protection)?

## D. Hardware
17. ★ NFC stand supplier, unit cost, order lead time, and who programs/prints the units?
18. Stand size and materials (acrylic, wood)? Branded per site or generic TapIt plus site logo?

## E. POPIA & operations
19. ★ Who is the Information Officer, and is registration with the Information Regulator done?
20. What guest data do we need at pilot: none (anonymous analytics), or email/phone for passport and offers?
21. Who provides the privacy policy and terms, or do we draft them for legal review?
22. Support: who answers a guesthouse owner when something breaks, and what hours?

## F. Technical
23. ★ VPS spec/provider/location (ZA data residency preferred). Is Portainer already installed?
24. ★ Who controls DNS for tapit.info and can it point to the VPS?
25. Email provider for system mail (transactional)? Any existing mailbox or domain setup?
26. Which payment gateway does the client already have an account with?
27. Are there existing booking systems operators use (Checkfront, Bookeo, FareHarbor, other) that we should integrate with?
28. Is there a budget for map/tile services and for an external penetration test?

## G. 3D / "adventure" ambition
29. ★ 3D = replaying a trip over 3D terrain plus phone tracking (not a 3D UI). Pilot gets a featured-trail flyover from admin-uploaded GPX; user tracking is phase 2. Agreed?
30. Do we have GPX/route data for the trails, and are the rights cleared?
